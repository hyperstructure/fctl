---
title: What a box costs
summary: Where the money in a 349 kr box goes.
date: 2026-05-02
---

People ask why a box costs 349 kr. Here's where it goes.

The farms get 41%. That's more than a supermarket pays, and it's the point.
Packing and the cold room take 14%. The vans, their drivers and their fuel
take 22%. Payments, software and the office take 9%.

That leaves 14%. Some of it pays back the loans for the vans. The rest is
what keeps us going through January, when nothing grows.

We publish this once a year. If a number moves, we'll say why.
